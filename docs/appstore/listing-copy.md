## Description

Progression is a training partner for people who take building muscle seriously. It plans your training in 13-week cycles, prescribes every set, and logs your workouts in seconds — so each session builds on the last.

START TRAINING IN ONE TAP
Pick the ready-made upper/lower program, build your own from the exercise catalog, or answer a few questions and let the AI coach design a personalized program you can refine in chat.

LOG WORKOUTS, NOT SPREADSHEETS
• Every exercise arrives with target sets, rep ranges, effort targets, and rest — weights progress automatically as you log
• Set-by-set logging built for the gym floor: big touch targets, no fiddling
• 13-week progression with three phases and a planned deload

KNOW YOU'RE PROGRESSING
• Insights computed from your actual training: strength trends on your main lifts, PRs, weekly volume vs muscle targets, streaks, and body-weight trend
• Personal records detected automatically — weight, reps, estimated 1RM, and session volume
• Full training history, week by week

RECOVER AS HARD AS YOU TRAIN
Connect WHOOP or Fitbit (optional) and the daily coach reads your recovery each morning — on rough days it suggests backing off instead of pushing. Your briefs reference real sleep and strain, and weight recommendations adapt to readiness.

TRACK THE WHOLE PICTURE
• Body weight and measurements
• Progress photos, captured with the camera and stored privately
• Daily coach briefs and workout reminders by push notification

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no social feed, no selling your data. Your training data belongs to you — and you can delete your account and everything with it, in the app, at any time.

Whether you're on your first program or your fifteenth cycle, Progression keeps the plan honest and the progress visible.

## App Review Information → Notes
Sign in with the demo credentials provided (username/password — no email needed). The account is pre-loaded with a training program and workout history.

Core flow: Home shows the dashboard → tap "Start Workout" to log sets → the Analytics tab shows computed insights.

Native features: camera capture for progress photos (Photos tab), daily push-notification coach briefs and reminders.

"Connected services" (WHOOP / Google Health) are optional wearable data integrations, not login methods — the app's only sign-in is username/password, so Sign in with Apple is not required.
